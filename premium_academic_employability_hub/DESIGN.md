## Brand & Style

The brand personality embodies academic ambition, career readiness, and modern African digital excellence. Designed for university undergraduates, postgraduates, and campus talent recruiters, the design language communicates institutional trust paired with high-velocity SaaS professionalism. The UI evokes confidence, motivation, and effortless clarity—transforming complex degree curricula and extracurricular milestones into a tangible pathway to employment.

Drawing inspiration from high-end modern SaaS platforms and progressive educational portals, the aesthetic balances crisp structured geometry with rich, energetic accents. Deep midnight navy anchors persistent navigation and deep surfaces, while pure white cards sit over a soothing light lavender canvas. Rich gradients of royal purple and electric violet command attention across key conversion funnels, milestone cards, and achievement states.

## Colors

The palette establishes an authoritative, focused atmosphere through calibrated deep tones and vibrant energetic violet accents.

- **Primary Anchor (`#14162F`):** Deep navy used for structural navigation rails, dark mode surfaces, high-contrast modal backings, and primary heading typography.
- **Secondary Core (`#4315D1`):** Deep royal purple utilized for primary buttons, active indicator accents, and foundational branding elements.
- **Tertiary Accent (`#6429EF`):** Vibrant electric violet applied in linear gradients (`linear-gradient(135deg, #4315D1 0%, #6429EF 100%)`), badge highlights, interactive focus states, and primary call-to-action hover layers.
- **Background Neutral (`#F7F6FF`):** Very light lavender canvas tint that softens glare and cleanly isolates pure white (`#FFFFFF`) card containers.
- **Muted Neutral (`#73758E`):** Mid-tone slate grey dedicated to secondary body copy, breadcrumbs, placeholder text, and subheaders.
- **Borders & Dividers (`#E7E5F5`):** Subtle soft lavender-tinted borders establishing clean compartmentalization without visual clutter.
- **Semantic Accents:**
  - Emerald Green (`#10B981`): Course completions, verified certifications, online status.
  - Amber Orange (`#F59E0B`): Learning streaks, time-sensitive campus job deadlines, attention badges.

## Typography

Typography establishes an immediate hierarchy of modern fintech and SaaS grade clarity. 

- **Headlines (Manrope):** Geometric structure, tight optical apertures, and assertive letterforms bring immediate authority to page titles, metric displays, and marketing lead-ins. High-order metrics and counters leverage tight letter spacing (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) for an athletic, confident posture.
- **Body & Functional UI (Hanken Grotesk):** Clean grotesk proportions optimize legibility across high-density data tables, student portfolios, course modules, and skill tag systems.
- **Editorial Emphasis:** Key display headings on public marketing flows pair bold Manrope headings with an italicized, violet-gradient accent word (e.g., *"A Degree is just the **Beginning...***") to spark inspiration and contrast against technical content.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout operates on an 8-point baseline grid with a fluid 12-column core for internal content workspaces and responsive desktop layouts.

- **Workspace & Dashboard Architecture:** Uses a fixed-width dark navy sidebar navigation (260px desktop width, collapsible into a slide-over drawer on mobile/tablet) paired with an expansive fluid content viewport.
- **Horizontal & Vertical Rhythm:** Card containers adhere to internal paddings of `space-lg` (24px) for prominent dashboards and `space-md` (16px) for compact status tiles. 
- **Responsive Adaptations:**
  - **Desktop (≥ 1280px):** 12-column layout with 24px gutters (`gutter-lg`) and 40px outer canvas margins. Multi-column dashboards split into a central learning feed (8 cols) and an auxiliary profile/opportunities rail (4 cols).
  - **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8-column layout with 16px gutters (`gutter`). Right-hand profile rails wrap below primary dashboard widgets.
  - **Mobile (≤ 767px):** 4-column single-flow layout with 16px margins (`margin-mobile`). Sidebar converts into a docked bottom navigation bar or an off-canvas drawer. Metric stat rows transition to 2-column grids or horizontal swipe tracks.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through high-contrast structural containment and diffused, low-opacity ambient shadows, avoiding harsh muddy blacks.

- **Canvas Base:** Flat `#F7F6FF` background creating a neutral base for white elevated cards.
- **Level 1 (Card Surfaces):** Pure white `#FFFFFF` paired with an ultra-thin border (`1px solid #E7E5F5`) and a diffused atmospheric drop shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(20, 22, 47, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Cards & Popovers):** Elevated hover state for courses, talent profiles, and active opportunity cards: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(20, 22, 47, 0.08)`.
- **Level 3 (Modal Dialogs & Floating Drawers):** High-prominence overlays: `0 24px 48px -8px rgba(20, 22, 47, 0.16)`.
- **Gradient Luminescence:** Primary interactive banners (such as student welcome heroes) combine deep indigo tones (`#14162F`) with multi-stop radial highlights of `#4315D1` and `#6429EF` to create deep, luminous spatial stages without flat visual dead-ends.

## Shapes

The interface balances disciplined structural cards with friendly, fluid interactive shapes.

- **Primary Cards & Containers:** Follow roundedness level 2 (`1rem` / 16px base, scaling up to `1.25rem` / 20px for large dashboard hero blocks) to evoke a clean, modern SaaS feel.
- **Pill Philosophy for Micro-Interactions:** Action buttons, status badges, skill chips, search inputs, and progress indicators leverage maximum full-radius pill styling (`9999px`).
- **Media & Avatar Shapes:** Student portraits, course thumbnails, and partner enterprise logos sit within circular (`rounded-full`) or 16px rounded-square surfaces with subtle 1px internal strokes.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padding `12px 28px`. Background is secondary core `#4315D1` or the primary gradient (`linear-gradient(135deg, #4315D1 0%, #6429EF 100%)`). Text is pure white, semibold, accompanied by a trailing directional icon (`ArrowRight` or `ChevronRight`). Hover transitions slightly scale brightness with a soft purple drop shadow (`0 8px 16px rgba(67, 21, 209, 0.25)`).
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Transparent background with a `1.5px solid #E7E5F5` border or light purple fill (`#F0EDFF`), text color `#4315D1`.
- **Dark Navigation Buttons:** Inverted navigation pills inside the dark `#14162F` sidebar: inactive items use `#73758E` text on transparent backgrounds; the active item transitions to `#4315D1` with white typography and an inset icon.

### Chips & Skill Badges
- Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), height 28px–32px, padding `4px 12px`.
- Composed of an optional leading dot or micro-icon, paired with label-md text.
- Standard skill badge: soft purple wash `#F0EDFF` with `#4315D1` typography.
- Status badges: emerald green `#ECFDF5` background with `#047857` text for "Completed", and amber `#FFFBEB` background with `#B45309` text for "In Progress".

### Cards & Learning Pathway Tiles
- Background pure `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E7E5F5`, border-radius 16px to 20px.
- Internal layout includes category label, course title in `headline-sm`, partner/university metadata, and a custom linear progress tracker.
- **Progress Trackers:** 6px tall pill-shaped track with `#E7E5F5` background; filled progress uses `#4315D1` transitioning into `#6429EF`.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- Search inputs are pill-shaped (`rounded-full`) on top bars and landing pages, or 12px rounded in structured profile forms.
- Background uses subtle tint `#F7F6FF` or pure `#FFFFFF` with `1.5px solid #E7E5F5`.
- Leading search icon in `#73758E`; active focus states reveal a crisp `#6429EF` ring with zero outline displacement (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(100, 41, 239, 0.15)`).

### Lists & Opportunity Items
- Dividers use hairline strokes (`1px solid #F0EEFC`).
- Opportunity list items feature a 48px rounded-2xl square icon badge (representing job or club role), title, organization, post timestamp, and a secondary "Apply" pill button aligned right.

### Metric Counter Blocks
- Structured 16px white tiles featuring an icon contained inside a soft circular pastel background (blue, green, amber, or violet), followed by a bold stat counter (`stat-counter`) and a crisp secondary label (`label-md`).
